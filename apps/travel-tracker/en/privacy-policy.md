## Summary

Travel-Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
All recorded information stays on your device. There are no user accounts,
no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services embedded in the app.

## Location data

Travel-Tracker records your device's location for one purpose only: to draw
the route of a travel you are actively recording. Recording happens exclusively
between the moment you tap "Start Recording" and the moment you tap
"Stop Recording".

The app requests the "Always" location permission so that recording can
continue while the app is in the background or closed. Even with this
permission, location is only recorded while a recording that you started is
active.

All recorded locations are stored in a local database on your device.
They are never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app.

## Sharing and export

You can export a travel as a GPX file using the share function. Such an
export is entirely under your control: the data leaves your device only when
you actively share it, and only to the destination you choose (for example an
email, a messaging app, or a file storage). We have no access to exported
files.

## Apple Maps

Travel-Tracker uses Apple's MapKit framework to display your routes on a
map. When a map is shown, your device requests map data from Apple. This
communication is handled by Apple and is subject to
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
Travel-Tracker itself does not send your recorded routes to Apple; only the
map region being displayed is requested.

## Data retention and deletion

Your recorded travels remain on your device until you delete them. You can
delete individual travels in the app at any time (swipe to delete).
Uninstalling the app removes all recorded data. Note that if you have enabled
an iCloud or computer backup of your device, backups made by iOS may include
the app's data; these backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because Travel-Tracker does not collect or process any personal data on our
systems, there is no data about you that we could access, correct, export, or
delete. All data subject rights under the EU General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability,
and objection — can be exercised directly on your device as described above.
If you have any questions, you can contact us at the address below.

## Children's privacy

Travel-Tracker does not collect personal data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If future versions of the app change how data is handled, we will update
this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes
effect.
